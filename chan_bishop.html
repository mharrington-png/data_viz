Babies Crawl Study — Mosaic Plot

This interactive visualization explores whether babies born during higher temperatures tend to crawl earlier than babies born during lower temperatures. Temperatures are grouped into Low, Medium, and High categories, while crawling ages are grouped into 3‑month ranges.

Later Crawling (7–9 months)
Very Late Crawling (10–12 months)
Hover over each section to explore the number and proportion of babies in each category. The width of each temperature group represents its proportion in the sample, while the height of each colored segment represents the proportion of crawling ages within that group.
