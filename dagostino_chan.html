Which sensory-restriction treatment was linked to the largest drop in Pd T scores?

This visualization compares pre-treatment and post-treatment MMPI scores for 42 volunteer prisoners. The plotted change score is pre-treatment Pd T score − post-treatment Pd T score, so positive values show a decrease after treatment.

What is a Pd T score? Pd refers to the MMPI Psychopathic Deviate scale. In this project, it is used as a measure related to a person’s need for control or rebellion against control. Scores above 70 are considered significantly high.

This keeps the original graph visible and highlights the subjects whose post-treatment Pd T score is below 70.

Inference summary

A paired-difference t procedure can be used to estimate the mean change for each treatment, assuming the sample is appropriate for inference and the paired differences are reasonably close to normal.
